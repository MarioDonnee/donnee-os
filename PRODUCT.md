# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Donnée OS is used by Donnée operators, consultants and managers who need to coordinate clients, projects and tasks inside one operational workspace. The user is usually inside a work session, reviewing status, creating records, checking execution and keeping the consultancy workflow coherent.

The interface serves repeated operational use. It should support quick scanning, confident creation of records and clear movement between dashboard, clients, projects and tasks.

## Product Purpose

Donnée OS exists to be the internal operating system for Donnée. It centralizes clients, projects, tasks, dashboards, activity logs and metadata-driven workflows so the team can manage delivery with more intelligence and less fragmentation.

Success means the core product flow works end to end: create clients, link projects, create tasks, inspect operational metrics and preserve auditability through backend logs.

## Brand Personality

Premium, intelligent, grounded.

The product should feel like a well-made internal tool for a consulting company that builds intelligent systems. Calm enough for daily repeated use. Distinctive enough to feel like it belongs to Donnée specifically — not a reskinned SaaS template.

The visual identity is dark forest-green with a slate-purple accent. Not synthetic neon. Not corporate blue. Not purple-black AI-product cliché. The palette comes directly from the Donnée brand: Verde Pinho, Grafite, Roxo Ardósia, Off-White.

## Brand Colors

- **Grafite** `#1A1824` — deepest surface, sidebar, drawers.
- **Verde Pinho** `#0D2C24` — application shell background, the dominant environment.
- **Roxo Ardósia** `#6B5CA5` — the sole accent color: buttons, active states, brand symbol.
- **Off-White** `#F2EFE9` — all primary text, logo wordmark on dark backgrounds.

## Typography

- **Helvena** — primary typeface at all sizes. Fallback: "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

## Logo

The Donnée logo uses a D-shape with a four-pointed sparkle/star symbol. Available variants are in `/imgs`. Primary use: symbol + wordmark on Verde Pinho background. See DESIGN.md for usage rules.

## Anti-references

Do not make Donnée OS look like a default Bootstrap admin panel, a generic SaaS template, a CRM clone or a marketing landing page.

Avoid bright corporate blue dashboards, flat white enterprise tables, overdecorated AI-product gradients (purple neon, glowing orbs), glassmorphism panels and decorative visual effects that do not improve operational clarity.

Do not use the old purple-black color system (#050012, #7c2cff, #d77cff). That palette has been retired.

Do not expand the MVP into advanced AI, automation, billing, finance, ERP or complex workflow engines unless explicitly requested.

## Design Principles

1. **Operational intelligence first.** Every screen should make current work easier to scan, create or decide on.
2. **Premium without spectacle.** The interface is dark, atmospheric and distinctive, but the work surface must remain readable and restrained.
3. **API-driven truth.** Statuses, priorities and operational categories should come from backend metadata when endpoints exist.
4. **Incremental product flow.** Build clients, projects, tasks and dashboard workflows before auth, automation or advanced intelligence.
5. **Brand-grounded, not generic.** Use the Donnée palette (Verde Pinho, Grafite, Roxo Ardósia, Off-White) deliberately. Avoid default admin patterns and template-like composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA behavior for contrast, focus visibility and keyboard-friendly controls. Preserve clear labels, readable hierarchy and responsive layouts. Motion should be restrained and nonessential; users should not need animation to understand state.
